How to Use UIMS Key

Step 1: Pay for key

Step 2: Check email for UIMS key

Once your payment has been confirmed an alpha-numeric key will be sent to your email

Step 3: Open Mendeley add-on in MS Word

Step 4: Select Citation setting

Step 5: Select Change Citation Style

Step 6: Scroll down and select add Custom Citation

Step 7: Copy UIMS url below and paste in Mendeley, MS Word

Copy: https://uims.com.ng/v1/send_uims?apikey=

Step 5: Copy UIMS key from email and paste in Mendeley, MS Word and click update
